# Changelog

## 0.6.0 — Developer preview

- First public source and Mac developer package under the name Apple-Watch-Cursor (formerly WristPilot).
- Native standalone Watch app and Mac companion connected over local Bluetooth LE.
- Six-cue personal finger-to-thumb tap calibration.
- Four animated, automatically detected pointing sweeps using full 3D wrist orientation, including natural elbow-centered arcs.
- Single-tap left click, double-tap mouse-mode switching, primary-display-only control and Watch Recenter.
- One-time device trust, automatic reconnect, preview tap feedback and bounded local diagnostics.
- Setup, troubleshooting, development and hardware validation guides; MIT license.

Watch display dimming can still interrupt control. The Watch app requires development signing and installation with Xcode. The Mac developer download is ad hoc signed and unnotarized.
